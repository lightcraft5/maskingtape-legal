Privacy Policy

Pawwriting (an app for making handwritten-style lettering)

Last updated: October 24, 2026

日本語

In short: This app does not collect any information about you. The lettering is generated entirely on your device and is never sent to any server. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

1. Information We Collect

This app collects, transmits and stores no information, personal or otherwise. It has no way to identify you and there is no account to create.

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

The app stores only the words you type together with the identifier (ID) assigned to each of them. This is so that when you rework the same words and send them again, they replace the earlier version in the maskingtape app rather than piling up as duplicates. The lettering itself is not stored. Everything is removed when you delete the app.

3. Sharing With Other Apps

To hand a design over to the maskingtape app, the app uses a shared area on your device with other apps from the same developer. The only things shared are the design image written out when you tap “Send to maskingtape”, and details such as its name. Nothing is shared with third-party apps or services.

4. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

This app uses no analytics tools, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It embeds no third-party SDKs.

5. Children

This app is safe for users of any age. Because it collects no information at all, it does not collect personal information from children.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

There is no information to disclose, because none is collected.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
